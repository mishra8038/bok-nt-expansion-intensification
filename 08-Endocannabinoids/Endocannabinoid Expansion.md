# Endocannabinoid Expansion

Endocannabinoid expansion builds new eCB structure: it increases CB1 receptor density (more robust tone-setting capacity) and strengthens the retrograde signaling pathways that fine-tune all other NT systems. The goal is to create a brain that has a highly responsive eCB system without over-sedation or over-suppression of other systems.

## Protocol

The sequence follows the universal kinetic rules in [[Universal Kinetic Rules]]: intermittent antagonism to trigger upregulation, then growth stimulation, then dense influx.

1. **Intermittent antagonism (days 1–5).** Reduce eCB precursor loading for 2–3 days (e.g. skip Arachidonic Acid) to trigger homeostatic upregulation of CB1 receptors. This is the "starve to grow" phase.
2. **Growth stimulation (daily).** Lion's Mane 1000 mg + Uridine Monophosphate (UMP) 250–500 mg + DHA 500–1000 mg. These neurotrophic factors drive synaptogenesis at eCB synapses and strengthen the retrograde signaling pathways.
3. **Dense influx (days 6+).** Resume Arachidonic Acid 500–1000 mg + CBD 25–50 mg + CBG 10–25 mg. This floods the newly upregulated receptors with eCBs, locking in the structural gains.

## Cycle

- **Duration:** 4–6 weeks.
- **Off-cycle:** 1–2 weeks with no AA, CBD, or CBG.
- **Do not combine** with MAO inhibitors or strong stimulants in the same window — see [[Universal Kinetic Rules]] for the stimulant burnout axiom.
- **Monitor for over-sedation:** drowsiness, GI discomfort, or reduced motivation. If these occur, reduce the dose or take an off-cycle sooner.

## Safety

- AA is generally safe at 500–1000 mg, but can increase inflammation in sensitive individuals.
- The intermittent antagonism phase (days 1–5) should not exceed 3 days without eCB precursors, to avoid excessive down-regulation and impaired tone-setting.
- **Avoid combining** CBD with MAO inhibitors or strong stimulants without careful monitoring.
- The eCB system is a **tone-setting** system: the goal is to optimize, not maximize, eCB signaling.
