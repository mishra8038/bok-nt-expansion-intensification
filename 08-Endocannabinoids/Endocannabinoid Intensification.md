# Endocannabinoid Intensification

Endocannabinoid intensification reinforces existing eCB signaling: it enhances the tone-setting function of the eCB system, improves mood and stress regulation, and strengthens the retrograde signaling that fine-tunes all other NT systems. The goal is to optimize eCB tone without over-sedation or over-suppression of other systems.

## Protocol

The sequence follows the universal kinetic rules in [[Universal Kinetic Rules]]: load the precursor first, then introduce the signal enhancer 60–90 minutes later.

1. **Load the precursor.** Arachidonic Acid (AA) 500–1000 mg (with a meal). This raises the intracellular AA pool and ensures the enzymes that synthesize 2-AG and anandamide are saturated.
2. **Introduce the signal enhancer (60–90 min later).** CBD 25–50 mg + CBG 10–25 mg. These phytocannabinoids inhibit FAAH (increasing anandamide) and modulate CB1 receptors, enhancing eCB tone.
3. **Optional: Serotonin co-loading.** If the goal is to enhance mood and reduce anxiety, add 5-HTP 50–100 mg + Kanna 300–600 mg with the precursor. This bridges into the [[Serotonin Intensification]] protocol.

## Cycle

- **Duration:** 4–6 weeks.
- **Off-cycle:** 1–2 weeks with no AA, CBD, or CBG.
- **Do not combine** with MAO inhibitors or strong stimulants in the same window — see [[Universal Kinetic Rules]] for the stimulant burnout axiom.
- **Monitor for over-sedation:** drowsiness, GI discomfort, or reduced motivation. If these occur, reduce the dose or take an off-cycle sooner.

## Safety

- AA is generally safe at 500–1000 mg, but can increase inflammation in sensitive individuals.
- CBD is generally safe at 25–50 mg. High doses (>100 mg) can cause drowsiness, GI discomfort, or liver enzyme elevation.
- CBG is generally safe at 10–25 mg.
- **Avoid combining** CBD with MAO inhibitors or strong stimulants without careful monitoring.
- The eCB system is a **tone-setting** system: the goal is to optimize, not maximize, eCB signaling.
