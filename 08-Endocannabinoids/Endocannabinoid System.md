# Endocannabinoid System

The endocannabinoid (eCB) system is a retrograde signaling system in which lipid-based messengers (endocannabinoids) are synthesized **on demand** in the postsynaptic neuron, travel backward across the synapse, and bind to cannabinoid receptors (CB1 and CB2) on the presynaptic terminal. It is the brain's primary **tone-setting** system: it modulates the release of every other neurotransmitter (glutamate, GABA, dopamine, serotonin, acetylcholine, norepinephrine) and is central to synaptic plasticity, mood, pain, appetite, and memory consolidation.

## Endocannabinoids

| Endocannabinoid | Primary Receptor | Role |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2-AG (2-arachidonoylglycerol) | CB1 | The most abundant eCB; modulates glutamate and GABA release; central to LTP and LTD |
| Anandamide (N-arachidonoyethanolamine) | CB1, CB2 | "Bliss molecule"; modulates mood, pain, and appetite |
| 2-AG and Anandamide are broken down by **MAGL** (monoacylglycerol lipase) and **FAAH** (fatty acid amide hydrolase), respectively |

## Receptors

| Receptor | Type | Location | Role |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| CB1 | G-protein coupled (Gi/o) | Presynaptic terminals (widespread) | Inhibits neurotransmitter release; the primary eCB receptor |
| CB2 | G-protein coupled (Gi/o) | Immune cells, glia, some neurons | Immune modulation, neuroprotection |
| GPR55 | G-protein coupled | Widespread | Modulates bone density, pain, and cognition |

## Roles

- **Retrograde signaling.** eCBs are the brain's primary retrograde messengers: they travel from postsynaptic to presynaptic, telling the presynaptic neuron to "release less" (or sometimes "release more"). This is the basis of **short-term depression (STD)** and **depolarization-induced suppression of inhibition (DSI)**.
- **Synaptic plasticity.** eCBs are critical for LTP and LTD. 2-AG is released during high-frequency stimulation and suppresses presynaptic glutamate release, contributing to the "fine-tuning" of synaptic strength.
- **Mood and stress.** The eCB system is a key regulator of mood and stress response. Low eCB tone is associated with anxiety, depression, and PTSD.
- **Pain modulation.** eCBs are the brain's primary endogenous analgesics; they suppress pain signaling in the spinal cord and brainstem.
- **Appetite and metabolism.** eCBs regulate hunger, satiety, and energy balance.
- **Memory consolidation.** eCBs in the hippocampus are critical for the consolidation of emotional and spatial memories.

## OTC / Supplement Agents

| Agent | Class | Role |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| CBD (Cannabidiol) | Phytocannabinoid | 5-HT1A PAM; indirect eCB modulator (inhibits FAAH, increasing anandamide) |
| CBG (Cannabigerol) | Phytocannabinoid | FAAH inhibitor; increases anandamide; anti-inflammatory |
| Arachidonic Acid (AA) | Fatty acid | Precursor for 2-AG and anandamide synthesis |
| Omega-3 (EPA/DHA) | Fatty acid | Competes with AA; modulates eCB synthesis and receptor sensitivity |
| Turmeric (Curcumin) | Polyphenol | FAAH inhibitor; increases anandamide; anti-inflammatory |
| Black Pepper (Piperine) | Alkaloid | Enhances bioavailability of CBD and other eCB modulators |

## Relationship to Other Systems

- **Glutamate.** eCBs suppress presynaptic glutamate release (STD); they are critical for the "fine-tuning" of LTP. The [[Glutamate Intensification]] and [[Glutamate Expansion]] protocols are modulated by eCB tone.
- **GABA.** eCBs suppress presynaptic GABA release (DSI); they are critical for the balance of excitation and inhibition. The [[GABA Intensification]] protocol is modulated by eCB tone.
- **Dopamine.** eCBs in the nucleus accumbens modulate dopamine release; they are central to reward and motivation. The [[Dopamine Intensification]] protocol is modulated by eCB tone.
- **Serotonin.** CBD is a 5-HT1A PAM; the eCB system and the serotonin system are tightly coupled in mood regulation. The [[Serotonin Intensification]] protocol is modulated by eCB tone.
- **Norepinephrine.** eCBs modulate NE release in the locus coeruleus; they are central to stress and arousal regulation. The [[Norepinephrine Intensification]] protocol is modulated by eCB tone.

## Safety

- CBD is generally safe at 25–50 mg. High doses (>100 mg) can cause drowsiness, GI discomfort, or liver enzyme elevation.
- CBG is generally safe at 10–25 mg.
- Arachidonic acid is generally safe at 500–1000 mg, but can increase inflammation in sensitive individuals.
- **Avoid combining** CBD with MAO inhibitors or strong stimulants without careful monitoring.
- The eCB system is a **tone-setting** system: the goal is to optimize, not maximize, eCB signaling.
