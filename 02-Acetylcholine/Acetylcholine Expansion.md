# Acetylcholine Expansion

Expansion of the cholinergic system means building new structure: more cholinergic receptor density, new dendritic spines, and novel attentional pathways. The mechanism is receptor upregulation, synaptogenesis, and neurogenesis. The agent sequence is *intermittent antagonist → neurotrophic growth stimulators → dense influx*, and the schedule below is built around the 4-to-6-week upregulation lag.

## The protocol

**Step 1 — Intermittent antagonist (deficit signal).** Briefly reduce cholinergic signaling so the neuron senses a deficit and responds by synthesizing fresh receptors. The framework names a choline-deprivation period. The OTC approach is a short, controlled reduction of choline intake (cutting back on Alpha-GPC/CDP-Choline for 3–5 days) combined with a mild muscarinic antagonist like **Areca nut extract** (used cautiously) or simply a low-choline diet phase.

**Step 2 — Neurotrophic growth stimulators.** Flood the upregulated system with growth factors that drive synaptogenesis and new spine formation.

**Step 3 — Dense influx.** Drive dense, well-timed cholinergic activity into the new architecture to wire it in.

## Stack schedule

| # | Agent | Class | Dose | Timing | Frequency | Cycle |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | Choline reduction (dietary) | Intermittent antagonist (deficit signal) | Cut Alpha-GPC/CDP-Choline to 0 | Days 1–5 | 1×/cycle | 4–6 weeks |
| 2 | Lion's Mane (Hericium) | Herbal / BDNF + NGF growth stimulator | 1000 mg | Morning + evening | Daily | 4–6 weeks |
| 3 | Uridine Monophosphate (UMP) | Nucleotide / synaptogenesis | 250–500 mg | Morning | Daily | 4–6 weeks |
| 4 | DHA (Omega-3) | Fatty acid / membrane + synaptogenesis | 500–1000 mg | With food | Daily | 4–6 weeks |
| 5 | CDP-Choline (Citicoline) | Choline / dense influx support | 500 mg | Morning, days 6+ | Daily | 4–6 weeks |
| 6 | Noopept | Peptide / cholinergic + BDNF (OTC in some regions) | 10–20 mg | Morning, days 6+ | 3×/week | 4–6 weeks |

**Notes on the schedule.**
- This is a 4–6 week minimum protocol. The choline-deprivation phase (#1) runs for the first ~5 days, then you shift to the growth-stimulator phase (#2–#4) and the dense-influx phase (#5–#6).
- The choline-deprivation phase is the OTC substitute for the framework's prescription cholinergic antagonist. It is milder, so the cycle runs the full 4–6 weeks.
- Noopept is the OTC substitute for the framework's prescription cholinergic growth stimulators. It is weaker, so the frequency is 3×/week rather than daily.
- Take a 1–2 week off-cycle after the 4–6 week run to let the new spines consolidate and to avoid a cholinergic crisis.
