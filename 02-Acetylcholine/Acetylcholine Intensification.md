# Acetylcholine Intensification

Intensification (reinforcement) of the cholinergic system means sharpening attention and encoding: increasing the signal-to-noise ratio, deepening focus, and strengthening the encoding of new information. The agent sequence is *precursor → PAM/agonist → signal enhancer (breakdown inhibitor)*, and the schedule below is built to obey the half-life rule (choline precursor peaks 60–90 min before the AChE inhibitor) and to avoid a cholinergic crisis (no stacking multiple ACh boosters at full dose on the same day).

## The protocol

**Step 1 — Precursor loading.** Load the choline pool so acetylcholine synthase has raw material to work with.

**Step 2 — PAM / agonist.** Amplify the natural ACh signal at the nicotinic and muscarinic sites.

**Step 3 — Signal enhancer (breakdown inhibitor).** Slow the breakdown of ACh by acetylcholinesterase (AChE) so the signal lingers.

## Stack schedule

| # | Agent | Class | Dose | Timing | Frequency | Cycle |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | Alpha-GPC | PR (choline) | 300–600 mg | Morning, on empty stomach | Daily | 4–6 weeks |
| 2 | CDP-Choline (Citicoline) | PR (choline) | 250–500 mg | Morning, with #1 | Daily | 4–6 weeks |
| 3 | Galantamine | P1 (nicotinic) | 4–8 mg | 60–90 min after #1–#2 | 3×/week | 4–6 weeks |
| 4 | Huperzine A | BI (AChE inhibitor) | 50–100 µg | With #3 | 3×/week | 4–6 weeks |
| 5 | Bacopa Monnieri | A3 (herbal / ACh + BDNF) | 300 mg (55% bacosides) | Evening | Daily | 4–6 weeks |

**Notes on the schedule.**
- The half-life rule: take #1–#2 (choline precursors) first, then #3–#4 (the PAM + AChE inhibitor) 60–90 minutes later, at the peak of the choline load.
- **Do not** take Galantamine and Huperzine A at full dose on the same day as a high-dose choline load — that is the cholinergic crisis risk. The 3×/week frequency for #3–#4 keeps the total ACh load manageable.
- Huperzine A is the OTC herbal substitute for the framework's prescription AChE inhibitors. It is weaker, so the cycle runs the full 4–6 weeks.
- Bacopa is a slow-acting herbal that supports ACh and BDNF over weeks; it is the "reset" agent that keeps the system from over-firing.
- Take a 1–2 week off-cycle after the 4–6 week run to avoid tolerance and let the system reset.
