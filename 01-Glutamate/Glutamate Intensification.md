# Glutamate Intensification

Intensification (reinforcement) of the glutamate system means driving LTP and synaptic consolidation in existing, under-fired, or older memory traces. The goal is to increase synaptic weight: pull hidden AMPA receptors to the surface so the same input produces a stronger, faster response. The agent sequence is *precursor → PAM/agonist → signal enhancer (breakdown inhibitor)*, and the schedule below is built to obey the half-life rule (precursor peaks 60–90 min before the enhancer) and the stimulant burnout axiom (no strong stimulant in the same window as the NMDA/AMPA enhancers).

## The protocol

**Step 1 — Precursor loading.** Load the raw material for glutamate synthesis and the magnesium that keeps the NMDA gate healthy.

**Step 2 — PAM / agonist.** Amplify the natural glutamate signal at the AMPA and NMDA sites so the $Mg^{2+}$ plug is displaced more easily and CaMKII is activated.

**Step 3 — Signal enhancer (breakdown inhibitor).** Slow the clearance of glutamate so the signal lingers long enough to drive consolidation.

## Stack schedule

| # | Agent | Class | Dose | Timing | Frequency | Cycle |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | L-Glutamine | Amino acid (precursor) | 1–3 g | Morning, on empty stomach | Daily | 4–6 weeks |
| 2 | Magnesium L-Threonate | Mineral / NMDA PAM | 1.5–2 g (142 mg elemental Mg) | Morning, with #1 | Daily | 4–6 weeks |
| 3 | Piracetam | Racetam / AMPA PAM (OTC in many regions) | 1.2–2.4 g | 60–90 min after #1–#2 | 2×/day | 4–6 weeks |
| 4 | Lion's Mane (Hericium) | Herbal / BDNF + AMPA support | 500–1000 mg | With #3 | Daily | 4–6 weeks |
| 5 | Alpha-GPC | Choline / glutamate support | 300–600 mg | With #1 | Daily | 4–6 weeks |

**Notes on the schedule.**
- The half-life rule: take #1–#2 (precursor + magnesium) first, then #3 (the AMPA PAM) 60–90 minutes later, at the peak of the precursor load.
- Magnesium L-Threonate is the OTC substitute for the framework's Neboglamine (a prescription NMDA glycine-site enhancer). It is weaker, so the cycle runs the full 4–6 weeks.
- Piracetam is OTC in much of Europe and available as a supplement elsewhere; where it is not, Lion's Mane carries the AMPA/BDNF role.
- **Do not** combine this stack with a strong stimulant (high-dose caffeine, amphetamines, modafinil) in the same window — that is the stimulant burnout axiom. If you use caffeine, keep it to a single morning cup and separate it from the NMDA/AMPA enhancers by at least an hour.
- Run the cycle for 4–6 weeks, then take a 1–2 week off-cycle to avoid tolerance and let the network consolidate.
