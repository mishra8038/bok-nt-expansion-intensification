# The Dopamine System

Dopamine is the neurotransmitter of hedonic drive, exploratory behavior, motor control, and working-memory gating. It is not simply the "pleasure chemical" — it is the signal that marks what is *salient*, what is worth pursuing, and what is worth remembering. It operates through two receptor families: **D1/D5** (direct pathway, driving motivation and cAMP signaling) and **D2/D3** (indirect pathway, smoothing motor and focus pathways). This folder holds the two protocols for working with it.

The dopaminergic system is also the one most prone to tolerance and down-regulation: repeated high-dose stimulation causes the network to shrink its own receptors, which is why the schedules in this folder are built around careful dosing, clear off-cycles, and the 4-to-6-week upregulation lag for expansion.
