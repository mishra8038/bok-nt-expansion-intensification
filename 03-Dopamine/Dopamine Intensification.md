# Dopamine Intensification

Intensification (reinforcement) of the dopaminergic system means sharpening motivation, salience, and working-memory gating: making the signal that marks what is worth pursuing stronger and more precise. The agent sequence is *precursor → PAM/agonist → signal enhancer (breakdown inhibitor)*, and the schedule below is built to obey the half-life rule (L-Tyrosine peaks 60–90 min before the MAO-B inhibitor) and to avoid tolerance (clear off-cycles).

## The protocol

**Step 1 — Precursor loading.** Load the tyrosine pool so tyrosine hydroxylase has raw material to synthesize dopamine.

**Step 2 — PAM / agonist.** Amplify the natural dopamine signal at the D1/D5 and D2/D3 sites.

**Step 3 — Signal enhancer (breakdown inhibitor).** Slow the breakdown of dopamine by monoamine oxidase B (MAO-B) so the signal lingers.

## Stack schedule

| # | Agent | Class | Dose | Timing | Frequency | Cycle |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | L-Tyrosine | Amino acid (precursor) | 500–1000 mg | Morning, on empty stomach | Daily | 4–6 weeks |
| 2 | Mucuna Pruriens | Herbal / L-DOPA source (OTC) | 500–1000 mg (15% L-DOPA) | Morning, with #1 | 3×/week | 4–6 weeks |
| 3 | Vitex Agnus-Castus (Chasteberry) | Herbal / D2 PAM (OTC) | 400–800 mg | 60–90 min after #1–#2 | Daily | 4–6 weeks |
| 4 | Selegiline (OTC lozenge) | MAO-B inhibitor (OTC where available) | 1.5–4.5 mg | With #3 | 3×/week | 4–6 weeks |
| 5 | B-Vitamins (B6, B9, B12) | Vitamins / cofactors for dopamine synthesis | Standard RDA | With food | Daily | 4–6 weeks |

**Notes on the schedule.**
- The half-life rule: take #1 (L-Tyrosine) first, then #3–#4 (the D2 PAM + MAO-B inhibitor) 60–90 minutes later, at the peak of the tyrosine load.
- Mucuna Pruriens is the OTC herbal substitute for the framework's prescription L-DOPA. It is weaker and more variable, so the frequency is 3×/week rather than daily.
- Selegiline is available OTC as a lozenge in some regions (for Parkinson's); where it is not, the MAO-B role is carried by a low-tyramine diet and the B-vitamin cofactors.
- **Do not** combine this stack with a strong stimulant (amphetamines, modafinil) in the same window — that is the stimulant burnout axiom, and it applies doubly to dopamine.
- Take a 1–2 week off-cycle after the 4–6 week run to avoid tolerance and let the system reset.
