# GABA Intensification

Intensification (reinforcement) of the GABA system means sharpening inhibitory tone and anxiolysis: making the brake that filters distracting input and prevents excitotoxicity more precise and more available. The agent sequence is *precursor → PAM/agonist → signal enhancer (breakdown inhibitor)*, and the schedule below is built to obey the half-life rule (L-Theanine peaks 60–90 min before the PAM) and to avoid over-sedation (no stacking multiple GABA boosters at full dose on the same day).

## The protocol

**Step 1 — Precursor loading.** Load the glutamate pool (GABA is synthesized from glutamate) so GAD (glutamic acid decarboxylase) has raw material to synthesize GABA.

**Step 2 — PAM / agonist.** Amplify the natural GABA signal at the $GABA_A$ sites.

**Step 3 — Signal enhancer (breakdown inhibitor).** Slow the breakdown of GABA so the signal lingers.

## Stack schedule

| # | Agent | Class | Dose | Timing | Frequency | Cycle |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | L-Theanine | Amino acid (precursor) | 100–200 mg | Morning, on empty stomach | Daily | 4–6 weeks |
| 2 | Magnesium (Glycinate) | Mineral / GABA support + calming | 200–400 mg (elemental Mg) | Evening | Daily | 4–6 weeks |
| 3 | Baicalein (Scutellaria) | Herbal / $GABA_A$ PAM (OTC) | 100–200 mg | 60–90 min after #1 | 3×/week | 4–6 weeks |
| 4 | Apigenin (Chamomile) | Herbal / $GABA_A$ PAM (OTC) | 50–100 mg | With #3 | 3×/week | 4–6 weeks |
| 5 | Lemon Balm (Melissa) | Herbal / GABA-T inhibitor (OTC) | 300–600 mg | Evening | 3×/week | 4–6 weeks |

**Notes on the schedule.**
- The half-life rule: take #1 (L-Theanine) first, then #3–#4 (the $GABA_A$ PAMs) 60–90 minutes later, at the peak of the theanine load.
- **Do not** combine this stack with a benzodiazepine or any other GABA agonist — that is the over-sedation risk. The 3×/week frequency for #3–#5 keeps the total GABA load manageable.
- Baicalein and Apigenin are the OTC herbal substitutes for the framework's prescription $GABA_A$ PAMs. They are weaker, so the frequency is 3×/week rather than daily.
- Lemon Balm is the OTC herbal substitute for the framework's prescription GABA-T inhibitors. It is weaker, so the frequency is 3×/week rather than daily.
- Take a 1–2 week off-cycle after the 4–6 week run to avoid tolerance and let the system reset.
