# The Serotonin System

Serotonin (5-HT) is the neurotransmitter of emotional stability, behavioral flexibility, impulse control, and hippocampal neurogenesis. It is not simply the "mood chemical" — it is the signal that modulates how the brain responds to stress, how flexible behavior is, and how new neurons are integrated into the hippocampus. It operates through multiple receptor subtypes, with **5-HT1A** (anxiolytic, driving quick calm under low baseline) and **5-HT2A/5-HT2C** (driving behavioral flexibility and downstream pattern shifts) being the most relevant to this framework. This folder holds the two protocols for working with it.

The serotonergic system is also the one most sensitive to over-stimulation: too much 5-HT at once produces serotonin syndrome (agitation, hyperthermia, tachycardia), so the schedules in this folder are built around careful dosing, clear off-cycles, and the 4-to-6-week upregulation lag for expansion.
