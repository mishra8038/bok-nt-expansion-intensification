# Serotonin Intensification

Intensification (reinforcement) of the serotonergic system means sharpening emotional stability, behavioral flexibility, and impulse control: making the signal that modulates stress response and mood more precise and more available. The agent sequence is *precursor → PAM/agonist → signal enhancer (breakdown inhibitor)*, and the schedule below is built to obey the half-life rule (5-HTP peaks 60–90 min before the reuptake blocker) and to avoid serotonin syndrome (no stacking multiple 5-HT boosters at full dose on the same day).

## The protocol

**Step 1 — Precursor loading.** Load the tryptophan pool so tryptophan hydroxylase has raw material to synthesize serotonin.

**Step 2 — PAM / agonist.** Amplify the natural serotonin signal at the 5-HT1A and 5-HT2A/2C sites.

**Step 3 — Signal enhancer (breakdown inhibitor).** Slow the reuptake of serotonin so the signal lingers.

## Stack schedule

| # | Agent | Class | Dose | Timing | Frequency | Cycle |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | 5-HTP | PR (amino acid) | 50–100 mg | Evening, on empty stomach | Daily | 4–6 weeks |
| 2 | L-Tryptophan | PR (amino acid) | 500–1000 mg | Evening, with #1 | Daily | 4–6 weeks |
| 3 | CBD (Cannabidiol) | P1 (5-HT1A) | 25–50 mg | 60–90 min after #1–#2 | 3×/week | 4–6 weeks |
| 4 | Kanna (Sceletium) | RI (SERT reuptake blocker) | 300–600 mg | With #3 | 3×/week | 4–6 weeks |
| 5 | B-Vitamins (B6, B9, B12) | CO (vitamins / cofactors) | Standard RDA | With food | Daily | 4–6 weeks |

**Notes on the schedule.**
- The half-life rule: take #1–#2 (5-HTP + L-Tryptophan) first, then #3–#4 (the 5-HT1A PAM + reuptake blocker) 60–90 minutes later, at the peak of the tryptophan load.
- **Do not** combine this stack with an SSRI, MAOI, or any other 5-HT booster — that is the serotonin syndrome risk. The 3×/week frequency for #3–#4 keeps the total 5-HT load manageable.
- CBD is the OTC herbal substitute for the framework's prescription 5-HT1A PAMs. It is weaker, so the frequency is 3×/week rather than daily.
- Kanna is the OTC herbal substitute for the framework's prescription SSRIs. It is much weaker, so the cycle runs the full 4–6 weeks.
- Take a 1–2 week off-cycle after the 4–6 week run to avoid tolerance and let the system reset.
