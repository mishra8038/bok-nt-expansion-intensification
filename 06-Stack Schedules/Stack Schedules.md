# Stack Schedules

This is the master reference for the frequency and timing of every stack in this book. Each neurotransmitter has two protocols — **Intensification** (reinforce) and **Expansion** (expand) — and the tables below consolidate the agent, class, dose, timing, frequency, and cycle for each. The schedules are built to obey the three universal kinetic rules in [[Universal Kinetic Rules]]: the half-life rule (precursor peaks 60–90 min before the enhancer), the 4-to-6-week upregulation lag (expansion is slow), and the stimulant burnout axiom (no strong stimulant in the same window as heavy NMDA/AMPA or monoamine enhancers).

## Cross-System Rules

- **One system at a time.** Do not run two Intensification or two Expansion protocols in the same week. The homeostatic dial is shared across systems, and stacking two protocols at once produces unpredictable down-regulation.
- **Intensify first, then expand.** Run a 4–6 week Intensification cycle to strengthen existing synapses, then a 4–6 week Expansion cycle to build new structure. Do not run them concurrently.
- **Off-cycles are mandatory.** After every 4–6 week cycle, take a 1–2 week off-cycle with no agents from that system. This is how the network consolidates and avoids tolerance.
- **The stimulant burnout axiom applies globally.** Never combine any heavy NMDA/AMPA or monoamine enhancer with a strong stimulant (high-dose caffeine, amphetamines, modafinil) in the same window.
