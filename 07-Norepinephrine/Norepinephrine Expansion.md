# Norepinephrine Expansion

Norepinephrine expansion builds new noradrenergic structure: it increases β2 receptor density in the prefrontal cortex (more robust attention circuitry) and strengthens the locus coeruleus–prefrontal projection. The goal is to create a brain that has a highly responsive arousal–attention system without the anxiety and cognitive rigidity that come from chronic over-stimulation.

## Protocol

The sequence follows the universal kinetic rules in [[Universal Kinetic Rules]]: intermittent antagonism to trigger upregulation, then growth stimulation, then dense influx.

1. **Intermittent antagonism (days 1–5).** Reduce NE precursor loading for 2–3 days (e.g. skip L-Tyrosine and L-Phenylalanine) to trigger homeostatic upregulation of β2 receptors in the prefrontal cortex. This is the "starve to grow" phase.
2. **Growth stimulation (daily).** Lion's Mane 1000 mg + Uridine Monophosphate (UMP) 250–500 mg + DHA 500–1000 mg. These neurotrophic factors drive synaptogenesis at noradrenergic synapses and strengthen the locus coeruleus–prefrontal projection.
3. **Dense influx (days 6+).** Resume L-Tyrosine 500–1000 mg + Rhodiola Rosea 300–600 mg + Panax Ginseng 200–400 mg. This floods the newly upregulated receptors with NE, locking in the structural gains.

## Cycle

- **Duration:** 4–6 weeks.
- **Off-cycle:** 1–2 weeks with no tyrosine, phenylalanine, or adaptogens.
- **Do not combine** with MAO inhibitors or strong stimulants in the same window — see [[Universal Kinetic Rules]] for the stimulant burnout axiom.
- **Monitor for over-stimulation:** anxiety, insomnia, elevated heart rate, or blood pressure. If these occur, reduce the dose or take an off-cycle sooner.

## Safety

- L-Tyrosine and L-Phenylalanine are generally safe at 500–1000 mg.
- The intermittent antagonism phase (days 1–5) should not exceed 3 days without NE precursors, to avoid excessive down-regulation and impaired attention.
- **Avoid combining** with MAO inhibitors or strong stimulants without careful monitoring.
- The inverted-U relationship means that **more is not better**: the goal is to optimize, not maximize, NE signaling.
