# Norepinephrine Intensification

Norepinephrine intensification reinforces existing noradrenergic signaling: it sharpens attention, enhances the signal-to-noise ratio in prefrontal cortex, and strengthens the arousal–attention circuit without triggering the anxiety and cognitive rigidity that come from over-stimulation. The goal is to operate at the **peak of the inverted-U**, not beyond it.

## Protocol

The sequence follows the universal kinetic rules in [[Universal Kinetic Rules]]: load the precursor first, then introduce the signal enhancer 60–90 minutes later.

1. **Load the precursor.** L-Tyrosine 500–1000 mg (fasted, morning). This raises the intracellular tyrosine pool and ensures the rate-limiting enzyme (tyrosine hydroxylase) is saturated. L-Phenylalanine 500 mg can be added for additional precursor support.
2. **Introduce the signal enhancer (60–90 min later).** Rhodiola Rosea 300–600 mg (standardized to 3% rosavins) + Panax Ginseng 200–400 mg (standardized to 5% ginsenosides). These adaptogens modulate NE release and reduce the stress response, keeping NE at the optimal level.
3. **Optional: ACh co-loading.** If the goal is to enhance attention and encoding, add Alpha-GPC 300 mg + CDP-Choline 250 mg with the precursor. This bridges into the [[Acetylcholine Intensification]] protocol.

## Cycle

- **Duration:** 4–6 weeks.
- **Off-cycle:** 1–2 weeks with no tyrosine, phenylalanine, or adaptogens.
- **Do not combine** with MAO inhibitors (e.g. Selegiline) or strong stimulants (amphetamines, modafinil) in the same window — see [[Universal Kinetic Rules]] for the stimulant burnout axiom.
- **Monitor for over-stimulation:** anxiety, insomnia, elevated heart rate, or blood pressure. If these occur, reduce the dose or take an off-cycle sooner.

## Safety

- L-Tyrosine and L-Phenylalanine are generally safe at 500–1000 mg.
- Rhodiola and Ginseng are generally safe at the doses listed, but can cause mild GI discomfort or insomnia in sensitive individuals.
- **Avoid combining** with MAO inhibitors or strong stimulants without careful monitoring.
- The inverted-U relationship means that **more is not better**: the goal is to optimize, not maximize, NE signaling.
